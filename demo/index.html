Formboard XR · recorded on Meta Quest 3

Wire harness assembly training in XR, driven by KBL data

A real VW harness from its KBL file, flattened 1:1 onto a virtual formboard. You route it wire by wire with your bare hands, and every peg is checked. At the end you see the finished formboard, and where the harness sits in the car.

Try it in the browser or on a headset Source on GitHub
VR

Virtual reality: in a virtual workshop

Press Enter VR: a workshop with a bench and a work light; teleport or walk with the sticks.

Wiring by hand

Pinch the wire end and pull it along the pegs. The wire grows to your fingertips, and each correct peg snaps green with a click.

Finished formboard

The whole run: the menu (harness, variant, mode), setting the guide pegs, Show me drawing the route, a wrong peg flashing red, the last wire, the quality check, and the summary with time, errors and hints per wire.

Real KBL data from an example car

The public VW Old Beetle harness from its KBL file, at 1:1: 183 segments from the KBL centre lines. Orange is the engine-compartment ignition harness that gets built on the formboard, shown where it sits in the car.

MR

Mixed reality: on a real table

Press Enter MR: the headset cameras show your room, and the formboard stands on your real table. The car harness lies at 1:1 on the floor. Captions in English and German.

Pegs and the first wires

Pinch the guide pegs into the board, then route the first wires. Each correct peg snaps green, with a click and a haptic tick.

Dense branches, wire by wire

Through the busy branch area to the last wire. The next peg glows, and the bundles build up as on a real formboard.

Finished board and the car at 1:1

The complete harness, the quality check, and the VW vehicle harness on the floor of the room, with the built harness in orange.
